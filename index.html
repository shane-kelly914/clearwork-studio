    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    What happens on the intro call?

    We talk for 30 minutes about your business and what's getting in the way. No strings attached and no payment needed. If there's a fit, the next step is the full interview.

    Do I need to know anything technical?

    No. You know your business; that's the part I need from you. I handle the technology, security and setup.

    Who owns what gets built?

    You do. The finished tool is configured for your business and registered in your name.

    How fast is the test model, really?

    48 to 72 hours after the interview. It's a working first version you can use and react to, which we then fine-tune together.

    How do you handle security?

    Security is built in from the start, not bolted on. I work as a cybersecurity analyst, so access control, data protection and safe defaults are part of every build.

    What if I only have an idea?

    That's a great place to start. The interview turns an idea into a clear list of what to build first, and the test model makes it real quickly.
